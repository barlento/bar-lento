3. Confidential information

Everything inside the application is confidential: schedules, names, clock-ins, hours, personal statistics, signed documents and any other record. It may be used only for Bar Lento's operations and must not be shared, copied or disclosed to anyone outside Bar Lento.

4. Personal data and privacy

  • What is stored: your name as it appears on the schedule; your 4-digit PIN, stored only as a salted cryptographic hash (the PIN itself is never stored and cannot be recovered); a random token identifying each phone or computer you signed in from; the email address you use to sign documents; the date, time and text fingerprint of every document you acknowledge; your scheduled shifts and confirmations; your clock-ins and clock-outs (mirrored read-only from the Toast POS, or recorded in the app for people who are not in Toast); your birthday if the manager entered it.
  • Why: to organize shifts, verify hours worked, comply with New York State and New York City labor laws (written policies, acknowledgments, time records) and keep the records the law requires.
  • Who sees it: you see your own data. The time clock (who is clocked in, times) is visible to signed-in employees because it is operational information. The manager and the owners see everything needed to run the business. Nobody else.
  • How long: records that the law requires (schedules, time records, signed policies) are kept for as long as the law requires, also after you leave. When you leave, your access is closed and your upcoming shifts are removed; your past records are archived, never deleted.
  • Where: the application runs on Vercel and stores data on Upstash (databases located in the United States). Emails with your signed copies are sent through Resend from the barlentony.com domain. Clock-in data comes from Toast, Bar Lento's point-of-sale system, in read-only mode. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No advertising, no tracking, no sale of data to anyone.
  • Your rights: you can ask the owners at any time for a copy of your records, a correction of a wrong entry, or an explanation of how your data is used. Write to the contacts below.
